# K-Gem Semantic Design System

## Derived from Stitch Project ID: 16262780099871630081

This document serves as the "Source of Truth" for the K-Gem application design, aligned with the premium aesthetic identified in the Stitch Design System analysis.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

- **Aesthetic**: Premium, Curated, and Trustworthy.
- **Mood**: Muted elegance with a focus on "0.1% Secret" exclusivity.
- **Density**: Airy but information-rich, using generous margins and whitespace.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

- **Primary (Deep Muted Violet)**: `#6d4e73` - Used for primary actions, BRAND identity, and key accents.
- **Secondary (Dusty Rose)**: `#b5838d` - Used for secondary highlights and categorized paths.
- **Accent (Peach Glow)**: `#e5989b` - Used for attention-grabbing elements (e.g., "Trending").
- **Background (Onyx Plum)**: `#1a161c` - The deep, sophisticated dark background basis.
- **Text (Off-White)**: `#fcfafc` - High-contrast main text.
- **Muted Text (Silver Mauve)**: `#a699a6` - Secondary descriptions and labels.

## 3. Typography Rules

- **Primary Font**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` - Selected for its modern, clean, yet sophisticated character.
- **Scale**:
  - **H1 (Hero)**: Bold, 3rem+ with tight tracking.
  - **Body**: Regular, 1rem with relaxed line-height for readability.
  - **Labels**: Bold, uppercase with tracking (0.1em+) for a technical, premium feel.

## 4. Component Stylings

- **Cards (Glassmorphism)**:
  - **Background**: `rgba(45, 38, 48, 0.7)` (Onyx Plum with 70% opacity).
  - **Blur**: `12px` (Backdrop Filter).
  - **Rounding**: `24px` (`--radius-premium`).
  - **Border**: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Buttons**:
  - **Primary**: Solid Deep Muted Violet with subtle shadow glow.
  - **Secondary**: Outlined or semi-transparent glass style.
  - **Shape**: Generously rounded (`3xl` or pill-shaped).

## 5. Functional Design Patterns

- **Explore Map**: Central discovery mechanism utilizing map-first interaction.
- **Detail View**: Minimalist detail cards with high-quality imagery and "Local Insider" verification badges.
- **0.1% Gate**: Premium content is clearly marked with a "Secret" badge and sophisticated lock mechanism.
